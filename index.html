Merge Mint: Wealth Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

🔒 Merge Mint does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. We do not have accounts or logins. Your game progress stays on your device. The app displays ads via Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases processed securely through RevenueCat.

1. Overview

Merge Mint: Wealth Puzzle ("the app") is a tile-merging puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

By using Merge Mint, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Merge Mint does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account or login credentials
  • Precise location data
  • Contact lists or other on-device personal files

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Game progress, board state, and scores
  • Daily missions progress and streaks
  • Coins, power-ups, and other in-game resources
  • App settings and preferences (e.g., sound, language)

You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Merge Mint uses the following third-party services to display ads and process purchases. Please refer to their own privacy policies for details on how they handle data.

Google AdMob admob.google.com

Used to display in-app advertising. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads. Privacy Policy

RevenueCat revenuecat.com

Used to process and manage in-app purchases (e.g., remove ads, VIP bundle, power-up packs). RevenueCat receives purchase and device data needed to validate transactions. Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

Merge Mint displays ads through Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings, or remove ads entirely through the in-app purchase.

6. In-App Purchases

Merge Mint offers optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads or purchasing power-up bundles). These purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not have access to your payment card details.

7. Children's Privacy

Merge Mint does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or personal data entry from any user, regardless of age.

8. Future Changes

Future versions of the app may introduce additional features, such as an optional online leaderboard. If such a feature is added, this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance to describe what information (such as a nickname and an anonymous device identifier) would be involved.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
